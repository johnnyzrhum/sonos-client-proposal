Music services & personal music library

Streaming choices: Spotify, Pandora and Apple Music are supported. The client may connect one or several services in the Sonos app; the proposed equipment does not require any particular subscription.

Personal music collection: Apple Music with Sync Library can provide access to a personal uploaded/matched music collection. iTunes Match ↗ is Apple's separate music-matching/cloud-library option; iTunes Plus is a DRM-free purchase format, not a cloud-hosting service. Confirm access to any matched/uploaded tracks through the Apple Music service on Sonos before relying on it as the only library source.

Local alternative: Sonos can play a music folder shared from a Mac or network-attached storage device on the home network. Sonos music library setup ↗.

Subscriptions, concurrent-stream limits and cloud-library eligibility vary by provider and account.

Installation and operational notes

Each indoor speaker, Beam, and Sub Mini requires power. The Sonos Play uses a rechargeable battery and an indoor-only charging base. The Beam additionally requires HDMI to the Samsung TV. No speaker cables run between Sonos components.

Compatible music services stream to Sonos over the home network; the iPhone is a controller, not a required continuous audio source for integrated services. Group any combination of zones, or play different sources in different rooms subject to service account limits.

Color: All selected models are offered in black and white; confirm the chosen finish at checkout.
