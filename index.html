Your music

Streaming: Sonos works with Spotify, Pandora and Apple Music. Use one or all of them. You don't need any particular subscription.

Your CDs: I'll copy them into iTunes for you. Then there are two ways to play them on Sonos. One is Apple Music with its Sync Library feature, which puts your own music in the cloud (more about iTunes Match ↗). The other is below.

Or from your Mac: Sonos can play a music folder stored on your Mac, so no subscription is needed. Sonos music library setup ↗.

Each music service has its own rules and limits.

Setup and good-to-know

Each indoor speaker, the Beam and the Sub Mini plug into a wall outlet. The outdoor Play runs on a rechargeable battery. The Beam also needs one HDMI cable to your Samsung TV. No wires run between the speakers.

Music comes to the speakers over your Wi-Fi. Your iPhone is just the remote, so it can leave the house and the music keeps playing. You can play one song everywhere or something different in each room.

Color: Everything comes in black or white. Choose at checkout.
